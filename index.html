MP Test — Privacy Policy (last updated 19 August 2026)

MP Test ("the app") is a market-overview dashboard that displays publicly available financial information.

Data we collect: none. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information.

Data stored on your device. Your watchlist and display settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. They are never transmitted anywhere.

Network requests. To display market information, the app requests publicly available data over HTTPS from CoinGecko (cryptocurrency prices), alternative.me (the Crypto Fear & Greed Index), and Yahoo Finance (index, commodity, and currency data). These requests contain no personal information or identifiers beyond what any standard internet connection discloses (such as your IP address, visible to any server you connect to). No third-party SDKs are embedded in the app.

Children. The app is not directed at children.

Disclaimer. The app presents public market information for general reference only and does not provide financial advice.
